- Confirm every dynamic `req.reject(message, field)` branch in `bug-write.js`,
  `drafts.js` and `permissions.js` maps to one of the business validation families
  above or receives a new stable row.
- Confirm i18n labels/headings are not incorrectly classified as error messages.

  mapped text may be user-facing.

  representative messages.
